# Privacy Policy for Wake Up Refreshed: Smart Alarm
### Effective Date: September 5, 2026

### Last Updated: September 5, 2026

Wake Up Refreshed: Smart Alarm ("we," "our," or "the App") is an indie, cross-device sleep tracking and smart alarm ecosystem designed for Android mobile devices and Wear OS smartwatches. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles your information. In short: your sensor, biometric, and sleep data are processed entirely on your local devices and are never uploaded to remote servers, sold, or shared with third parties.

## 1. Information We Access and Collect
To provide smart alarm functionality, the App requests permission to access certain hardware sensors and local device storage.

## A. Health, Biometric & Body Sensor Data (Wear OS)
Data Accessed: Real-time heart rate measurements and accelerometer (motion) data.

How It Is Used: The App processes this data in real-time on your smartwatch using epoch-based scoring algorithms to calculate your baseline heart rate and detect transitions into "light sleep." This allows the App to trigger your alarm during your designated Smart Monitoring Window.

Storage & Transmission: Sensor measurements are processed in temporary memory on your Wear OS device. They are never uploaded to external cloud servers.

## B. App Preferences and Alarm Data
Data Collected: Alarm target times, labels, days of the week, vibration/ring preferences, and Smart Window duration settings.

How It Is Used: Stored locally on your Android phone using an on-device database to ensure your alarms ring reliably at the intended times.

## C. Local Debug & Diagnostics Logs
Data Collected: Optional sensor summary metrics and decision logs.

How It Is Used: If you activate Debug Mode, diagnostic logs are generated and stored locally on your device storage to help troubleshoot hardware sync or algorithm performance.

Control: These logs stay on your device unless you explicitly tap the "Share Debug Logs" button to email or transfer them yourself.

## 2. How Data Is Transferred (Cross-Device Synchronization)
The App synchronization between your Wear OS smartwatch and Android phone utilizes the Google Play Services Wearable Data Layer API.

All communication between your phone and watch occurs locally over secure Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi pairing.

No data passes through external developer servers or third-party cloud infrastructure.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Analytics
No Analytics SDKs: We do not use third-party analytics tracking tools (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Google Analytics).

No Advertising: The App is completely ad-free. We do not integrate any third-party ad networks or tracking frameworks.

No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal, sensor, or biometric data to third parties under any circumstances.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
Local Storage: All app preferences, local alarm database records, and debug logs remain on your local phone and watch storage for as long as the App is installed.

Data Deletion: You can delete all app data at any time by clearing the App's storage via your phone's system settings (Settings > Apps > Wake Up Refreshed > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the App from your phone and smartwatch. Uninstalling completely removes all stored database files and logs.

## 5. Permissions
The App requests the following key Android and Wear OS permissions:

Body Sensors / Health Services (BODY_SENSORS): Required on Wear OS to measure heart rate baseline shifts during sleep.

High-Sampling Rate Sensors (HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS): Required to process micro-movements via the smartwatch accelerometer.

Notifications & Wake Locks: Required to ring the alarm interface while the phone or watch is asleep or in Doze mode.

Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to ensure alarms trigger at precise times.

## 6. Health & Medical Disclaimer
Wake Up Refreshed: Smart Alarm is a lifestyle and comfort utility intended strictly for personal timekeeping and wellness support. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any sleep disorder, heart condition, or medical ailment.

## 7. Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Because all data remains local on the user's physical device, no personal data from children is collected or transmitted.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date."
